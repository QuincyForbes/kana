Seven times the chart lies to you

は
As a particle it's read wa, not ha. こんにちは → konnichiwa
へ
As a particle it's read e, not he. えきへ → eki e
を
Always o. It only ever appears as a particle.
っ／ッ
Small tsu. Doubles the next consonant, one beat of silence. けっこう → kekkou
ー
Katakana only. Stretches the vowel before it. コーヒー → kōhī
ゃゅょ
Small — they merge with the kana before. き + ゃ = kya, two letters, one beat.
です
The final u nearly vanishes. です sounds like des, ます like mas.

Using this

Read a phrase box by box out loud before reading it as a word. Tap 🔊 to hear it. Then live in the Quiz tab: single characters, phrases and kanji are all separate decks, or take everything at once. Type-the-romaji mode checks your answer and accepts both Hepburn and Kunrei spelling (shi/si, tsu/tu). Misses come back within minutes; hits come back in days. Ten minutes a day beats an hour on Sunday.

Suggested order: Hiragana and Katakana character decks in typed mode until they're automatic, katakana loanwords next, then phrases, kanji throughout. Progress shows exactly where you stand per deck.
